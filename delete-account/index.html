Lunomi Account Deletion

Last updated: 31 August 2026

You can delete your Lunomi account in two ways: from inside the app, or by sending us an email. You do not need to reinstall Lunomi to request deletion.

1. Delete your account inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it is immediate.

  1. Open Lunomi and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to your account page — the Profile button on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm when you are asked. The account is deleted and you are signed out.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, or you have already uninstalled it, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Request Account Deletion

The button opens your email app with the subject Lunomi Account Deletion Request already filled in. If it does not work on your device, write to lunomi.app@gmail.com using that subject line.

So that we can find the right account

Please send the request from the same Google email address you use to sign in to Lunomi. That is how we identify which account to delete, and it is the method we ask for.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write to us anyway and say so. We will work out an appropriate way to establish that the account is yours before anything is deleted. We cannot delete an account on the strength of a username and a different email address alone, because that is not evidence that the account belongs to the person asking — and deleting the wrong account cannot be undone.

We will never ask you for a password, a Google password, a verification code, or any other authentication credential. Please do not send any of these to us. Nobody asking for them is acting on Lunomi's behalf.

What deletion covers

Deleting your Lunomi account is intended to delete the account and its associated server-side Lunomi data, including where applicable:

  • your Supabase authentication account;
  • your public Lunomi username and profile;
  • your friendships and pending friend requests;
  • your Compete and challenge results;
  • your streak information;
  • your podium and achievement counts, and related online progress data.

What stays on your device

Lunomi keeps some learning and application data locally on your device so that it works offline — your settings, the decks and cards you made, and your progress in the games. This data is not on our servers and is not affected by deleting your account.

It is removed when you clear Lunomi's app data in your phone's settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Before you delete

Account deletion cannot be undone. Signing in again afterwards with the same Google account creates a new, empty Lunomi account: the username, friends, streaks and results from the old one are gone.

Questions

For anything else, see the Privacy Policy or the Support page, or email lunomi.app@gmail.com.
