Lunomi

Lunomi is a multilingual vocabulary-learning mobile application. It teaches words through picture flashcards, practice exercises and short games.

Much of Lunomi works without an account. An optional account — created with Google Sign-In — adds the online parts: a public profile, friends, and the daily Compete challenge.

Lunomi has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
