Support

Lunomi is supported by email. There is no form to fill in and no account needed to write to us.

Email us

Contact Lunomi Support

Or write directly to lunomi.app@gmail.com.

What we can help with

  • Account and login problems — Google Sign-In not working, or trouble choosing a username.
  • Technical issues — something not working, a game behaving oddly, missing pictures or sound.
  • Privacy questions — anything about the Privacy Policy or the data held for your account.
  • Account deletion — see the Delete Account page, which covers both the in-app and the email route.
  • General feedback — ideas, corrections to a translation, or a word that is wrong.

What helps us answer quickly

If you are reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • what you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead;
  • your phone model and Android version;
  • the Lunomi version, if you know it;
  • a screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes or any other sign-in credentials. We never need them, and we will never ask for them.

Reply times

Lunomi is developed and published independently, and support is answered by a person rather than a helpdesk. We read every message and reply as soon as we reasonably can.
